Our Objective

Our objective is to support human artists against AI in creative works, films, animation and art. As well as to show how AI in industry use is harmful to creative works and how it can butcher an artwork, to spread awareness that AI steals artworks from many artsits around the world in order to train itself. Our site is not meant to mindelessly refuse AI as a technological advancement but to spread awareness, support human artists and go against Generative AI in creative works and industry use.

Our Mission

The goal of our website is to raise awareness about the effects of generative AI on artists and creative people across many industries. We are aiming to educate and raise awareness to the users that generative AI shouldn’t replace creatives and artists in industries, and how generative AI has stolen and trained itself from various artworks without the consent of many artists around the world. The website isn’t against AI itself, but generative AI being used to replace artists.

Our Vision

Our vision is to support human artists and creatives in the creative industry, and to raise awareness about the effects of generative AI on artists and creatives. We want to educate users about how generative AI has stolen and trained itself from various artworks without the consent of many artists around the world. We want to show how generative AI shouldn't replace creatives and artists in industries, and how it can butcher an artwork.
